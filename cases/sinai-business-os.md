## English

**Type:** Business management platform (SaaS)

### Overview
An all-in-one business operating system that brings a company's day-to-day operations into a single platform, so teams stop jumping between disconnected tools.

### What we delivered
- Product strategy and UX/UI design
- Web platform with role-based access
- Dashboards and management reports
- Integrations with the client's existing tools
